Sun hours for homes in Madrid: orientation, floor and season

In short. In Madrid (40.4° N) the sun climbs to 26.2° at noon on 21 December and 73° on 21 June. A south-facing window with a clear view can see the sun for 9.2 hours on the shortest day; a north-facing window gets none from the September to the March equinox and 6.8 hours on 21 June. On a 12 m street lined with 18 m buildings, a south-facing flat gets midday sun in December only from the 4th floor up.

Around the equinoxes the sun's angle across an east-west street stays almost constant all day, so a south-facing window tends to get sun for most of the day or not at all: the jump between floors is real, not a rounding artefact.

What this means for homes in Madrid

  • For winter sun, south-facing and high beats everything. A clear south-facing window sees the sun for 9.2 of the 9.2 daylight hours on 21 December; east or west sees 4.5.
  • On narrow streets, low floors lose the winter sun. On a 20 m street the south-facing floors get ground 0 h, 1st 0 h, 2nd 0 h, 3rd 5.1 h, 4th 7.3 h, 5th 8.7 h on 21 December.
  • Summer is generous to every orientation, including north (6.8 h with a clear view on 21 June).
  • In summer the strongest sun in Madrid arrives on west-facing windows in the afternoon, so shading matters as much as sun.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours of sun does a south-facing flat get in Madrid?

With nothing in front, a south-facing window in Madrid can see the sun for 9.2 hours on 21 December and 7.7 hours on 21 June. On a 12 m street lined with 18 m buildings, the ground floor gets 0 hours on 21 December and the 5th floor 8.2 hours.

Which floor gets winter sun in Madrid?

For a south-facing window opposite 18 m buildings, midday sun on 21 December starts at the 5th floor on an 8 m street, the 4th floor on a 12 m street, the 3rd floor on a 20 m street and the 1st floor on a 30 m street.

Does a north-facing flat in Madrid get any direct sun?

Only in the warmer half of the year: 6.8 hours on 21 June with a clear view, early in the morning and in the evening, and none between the September and March equinoxes.

When is the sun lowest in Madrid?

On 21 December the sun rises at 08:38 and sets at 17:48 local time, and reaches 26.2° above the horizon at 13:13.
